Georgia - published administrative guidance on immigration case handling
Checked 2026-11-15.

Result: no guidance for decision-makers is published. Procedure is set by legal acts, all
public on matsne.gov.ge: government resolutions (residence permits, work permits, stateless
status, visas), orders of the Minister of Internal Affairs (asylum requests, expulsion,
detention; the Ministry's Migration Department decides asylum and expulsion), orders of the
Minister of Justice and decisions of the Citizenship Commission (citizenship). The Public
Service Development Agency (SDA), which decides residence permits, citizenship and stateless
status, publishes only those acts and applicant information. No manual, instruction,
methodological recommendation or circular to officials was found.
