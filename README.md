# Immigration guidance: Georgia

Published guidance that the authorities of Georgia issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** 16 files in this folder. `index.tsv` lists each file with its title, date and source address.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` and `not-obtained.tsv` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

Georgia publishes no guidance for its officials. This repository holds 13 procedural acts from 2014 to 2026: government resolutions on residence permits, work permits, stateless status and visas, Citizenship Commission decisions, justice ministry orders, and the 2025 interior ministry orders on identifying asylum requests, expulsion and detention. The State Commission on Migration Issues' guidebooks could not be fetched: its site answers automated requests with a challenge.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
